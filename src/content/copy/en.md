## Hero

I'm May García, a self-taught artist inspired by the beauty of nature, the rhythm of music, and the magic of dreams. Each painting is a burst of color — a reflection of emotion and energy that speaks from the heart.

## A world of color

Dreams do come true, and life reveals its beauty when lived in full color. Through each painting I turn fragments of life and fleeting stories into art — echoes of the world translated through my color palette.

## Behind the canvas

Vibrant hues bring energy, joy, and a sense of fun to every piece. Nature's beauty serves as a constant muse, guiding my creative process, while abstract art remains my greatest influence — letting me express emotions and dreams in ways that words cannot.

## About

I'm May García, a Colombian artist passionate about creating works that connect emotions with spaces. Every piece I create carries a personal story — a moment captured in color and form.

My work combines contemporary techniques with influences from Colombian culture, creating a unique and recognizable style. I work primarily with acrylics, watercolors and mixed media.

Nature's colors, the rhythm of music, and the emotional pulse of humanity guide my process. I create to find peace amid the noise and invite others to feel that same pause through my work.
